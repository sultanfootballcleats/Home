Our Story

Genuine Boots,
Honest Prices

Sultan Football Cleats started with one question: why should a genuine pair of Nike, Adidas or Puma boots cost a season's wages? We source, condition-check and sell original pre-loved cleats directly, without the markup of a retail middleman.

Where It Started

From the Local Pitch
to a Full Catalog

Sultan began as a small collection of pre-loved boots, hand-picked and checked pair by pair. Every listing gets a real condition score and an honest note on any wear, so you know exactly what's on its way to you.

Today the catalog covers adult and kids' studs and grippers from Nike, Adidas, Puma and more, but the process hasn't changed. Each pair is a single unique unit, and we still talk directly to the players who wear them.
